# Schedule 1, Item 12 (Municipal Structures Act 117 of 1998): the council quota formula

Research date: 2026-11-01. Scope: South Africa; law relevant to the 4 Nov 2026 municipal elections.

Method note: the formula in the Act is an embedded image, so the text extraction drops it. I rendered the PDF page and read the image directly. The IEC report text was extracted from the PDFs with pdftotext.

## What is the verbatim wording of Item 12, including the definitions of A, B and C (and D if any)?

### Takeaway
The Act's Item 12 defines only A, B and C. The formula is A / (B - C) + 1, with "fractions to be disregarded". Item 12(2) doubles votes for unopposed ward candidates. I could NOT retrieve a post-2002 consolidation of the Act, so the verbatim text below is the LawLibrary consolidation "as at 5 August 2002". Act 3 of 2021 (the latest amending Act I found) does not touch Item 12.

### Cited Findings
- Version used: LawLibrary/AfricanLII consolidated PDF, "Legislation as at 5 August 2002", FRBR URI /akn/za/act/1998/117/eng@2002-08-05. The PDF itself carries a note that it "is not the latest available version". Item 12 carries no amendment annotation in it (compare the adjacent annotation at item 10: "[item 10 amended by section 93 of Act 27 of 2000 and substituted by section 8 of Act 20 of 2002]"). — [LawLibrary PDF, Act 117 of 1998 as at 5 Aug 2002](https://media.lawlibrary.org.za/media/legislation/298866/source_file/57c2031caec55db5/1998-117.pdf) (printed page 38, PDF page 43)
- Verbatim text of Item 12 in that version (the formula is an image; read from the rendered page as "A over (B–C), plus 1"):
  - "12. Quota (1) The quota of votes for a seat in the metro or local council, must be determined in accordance with the following formula (fractions to be disregarded):"
  - [formula image: A / (B–C) + 1]
  - "Where— A represents the total number of valid votes cast for all parties, consisting of those cast on the party vote and those cast for ward candidates representing parties; B represents, the number of seats in the metro or local council; and C represents the number of independent ward councillors elected in the election."
  - "(2) If a ward candidate representing a party is elected unopposed, a vote cast by a voter registered in that ward for the party of which that candidate is a representative must for the purpose of factor A be counted as two votes."
  — [LawLibrary PDF as at 5 Aug 2002](https://media.lawlibrary.org.za/media/legislation/298866/source_file/57c2031caec55db5/1998-117.pdf)
- The same Item 12 wording appears in the earlier LawLibrary consolidation as at 1 March 2001 (Item 12 text at the equivalent location, same A/B/C definitions), so Item 12 was stable across the two consolidations I checked. — [LawLibrary PDF as at 1 Mar 2001](https://media.lawlibrary.org.za/media/legislation/298633/source_file/f9f75921138d5e38/1998-117.pdf)
- Act 3 of 2021 (Municipal Structures Amendment Act, commenced 1 Nov 2021) amends Schedule 1 in its section 33, in items 16 (new subitems (3)-(9)), 17(1), new 17A, 18(1)(b) and (e), 20(1), and items 2 and 3 as I read the extracted text (not checked closely). A text search of the Act's text found no amendment to item 12. — [Act 3 of 2021 PDF (Corruption Watch copy)](https://www.corruptionwatch.org.za/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Act_3_of_2021_Municipal_Structures.pdf); commencement date from [Corruption Watch](https://www.corruptionwatch.org.za/municipal-structures-amendment-act-to-commence/)
- Electoral Laws Amendment Act 4 of 2021 is reported to amend the Electoral Commission Act and the Municipal Electoral Act, not the Structures Act. This is a search-result summary, not something I read in the Act. — [gov.za](https://www.gov.za/node/806907)

### Inferences
- Because no amendment I could find touches Item 12 after 2002, the 2002 wording is probably still the text in force. This is an inference, not confirmed against a current consolidation.

### Gaps
- No current (2021 to 2026) consolidated text of the Act could be retrieved. lawlibrary.org.za pages return HTTP 403 to this environment, the gov.za original (a117-980.pdf) reset the connection, and francesbaard.gov.za reset the connection. ## Does the Act text contain a "D" term? Where does D come from?

### Takeaway
No. Item 12 in the Act has only A, B and C. The D term, "Ward Councillors Seats from Parties with no PR List", appears only in the IEC's own Seat Calculation Detail reports.

### Cited Findings
- Item 12(1) defines only A, B and C (text above). — [LawLibrary PDF as at 5 Aug 2002](https://media.lawlibrary.org.za/media/legislation/298866/source_file/57c2031caec55db5/1998-117.pdf)
- A "D" does appear elsewhere in Schedule 1, but with a different meaning: item 14 subitem (4) (the forfeited-seats quota) defines "D represents the number of seats awarded to the forfeiting party" and "E represents the number of independent ward councillors elected". This is a different formula. — same PDF, item 14(4)
- The IEC reports (below) print a D line "Ward Councillors Seats from Parties with no PR List - (D)" and put it in the denominator. — [IEC NW405 2021 report](https://results.elections.org.za/home/LGEPublicReports/1091/Seat%20Calculation%20Detail/NW/NW405.pdf)

### Inferences
- D is an IEC operational adjustment, not in the Act's Item 12 text. I found no regulation or amendment that introduces it, and I did not find any IEC document explaining why D is subtracted. (Plausible rationale, not sourced: ward seats won by parties that have no PR list cannot be offset against a PR list, so they are removed from the seats shared out.)
- In every report I opened D = 0, so the Act formula and the IEC formula give identical quotas in those municipalities. The D variant only matters where a party with no PR list wins a ward.

### Gaps
- The legal basis for D is not found. Needs the IEC's legal opinion, a Commission regulation, or an IEC election-procedure document.

## Do the IEC's 2021 Seat Calculation Detail reports print Q = (A / (B - C - D)) + 1?

### Takeaway
Yes. Both 2021 reports I opened (JB Marks NW405 and Maluti a Phofung FS194), and the two 2016 reports (Bushbuckridge MP325, Ventersdorp/Tlokwe NW405), print "Formula : Q = (A / (B - C- D)) + 1; disregarding fractions. TVV: Total Valid Votes".

### Cited Findings
- JB Marks NW405, LGE 2021 (printed 2021/11/22 15:43:44): "Quota - (Q) 1,515 Formula : Q = (A / (B - C- D)) + 1; disregarding fractions. TVV: Total Valid Votes"; "Total Valid Votes Cast for All Parties - (A) 101,439"; "Total Seats Available in Municipality - (B) 67"; "Independent Ward Councillors Elected - (C) 0"; "Ward Councillors Seats from Parties with no PR List - (D) 0". — [IEC NW405 2021](https://results.elections.org.za/home/LGEPublicReports/1091/Seat%20Calculation%20Detail/NW/NW405.pdf)
- Maluti a Phofung FS194, LGE 2021 (printed 2021/11/22 16:04:54): Q 2,248; A 157,298; B 70; C 0; D 0; same formula string. — [IEC FS194 2021](https://results.elections.org.za/home/LGEPublicReports/1091/Seat%20Calculation%20Detail/FS/FS194.pdf)
- The same formula string appears on the 2016 reports (Bushbuckridge MP325, Ventersdorp/Tlokwe NW405), so D has been printed since at least 2016. — [IEC MP325 2016](https://www.elections.org.za/content/LGEPublicReports/402/Seat%20Calculation%20Detail/MP/MP325.pdf); [IEC NW405 2016](https://www.elections.org.za/content/LGEPublicReports/402/Seat%20Calculation%20Detail/NW/NW405.pdf)
- The IEC's own 2021 voter-education slides print a simpler version that has no D: "Quota = Total Valid Votes (Ward + PR) / (Total Seats - Independents) + 1", and say the result "plus one, disregarding fractions, is the quota". — [IEC 2021 LGE Electoral System slides](https://www.elections.org.za/content/Documents/Voter-education/Continuous-Voter-Education/How-Council-seats-are-calculated/)

### Inferences
- Question 1 of the objective: the statutory denominator is B - C. The IEC reports use B - C - D. The two differ only when D > 0.

### Gaps
- I did not find a 2021 report with D greater than zero. A scan of all 2021 municipalities for D > 0 was not done; it would show whether the D variant ever changed a quota.
- Reports from the 2026 election cycle do not exist yet (today is 2026-11-01).

## Is the quota truncated before adding 1? What does the text say about fractions?

### Takeaway
The Act says only "(fractions to be disregarded)" in the lead-in to the formula, with the "+1" inside the formula image; it does not separately say which step is truncated. Because 1 is an integer, floor(x) + 1 and floor(x + 1) are always equal, so the order makes no difference to the result.

### Cited Findings
- Act: "...in accordance with the following formula (fractions to be disregarded):" — [LawLibrary PDF as at 5 Aug 2002](https://media.lawlibrary.org.za/media/legislation/298866/source_file/57c2031caec55db5/1998-117.pdf)
- IEC: "Q = (A / (B - C- D)) + 1; disregarding fractions" — [IEC NW405 2021](https://results.elections.org.za/home/LGEPublicReports/1091/Seat%20Calculation%20Detail/NW/NW405.pdf)
- Arithmetic check against the IEC figures (my calculation): JB Marks 101,439 / 67 = 1,514.07, floor 1,514, plus 1 = 1,515, matching the printed Q. Maluti a Phofung 157,298 / 70 = 2,247.11, floor 2,247, plus 1 = 2,248, matching the printed Q.
- The IEC report also shows party "Total Valid Votes / Quota" to four decimals (JB Marks ANC 48,911 / 1,515 = 32.2845), so the quota used for dividing votes is the truncated integer and the party quotients are NOT truncated before the remainder is ranked; remainders (e.g. 0.2845) are ranked. This matches Item 13(2). — [IEC NW405 2021](https://results.elections.org.za/home/LGEPublicReports/1091/Seat%20Calculation%20Detail/NW/NW405.pdf)

### Inferences
- The "+1" is part of the statutory formula (it appears in the formula image), and truncation is stated for the whole quota. The text does not say the fraction is rounded, only "disregarded".

### Gaps
- The statute does not say what happens if the denominator is zero or negative; not relevant to normal councils.

## Does the Act say anything about unopposed ward candidates doubling votes for factor A?

### Takeaway
Yes. Item 12(2) doubles the votes for factor A. Item 13(1)(b) applies the same doubling to the per-party vote counts used in the seat allocation.

### Cited Findings
- Item 12(2), verbatim: "If a ward candidate representing a party is elected unopposed, a vote cast by a voter registered in that ward for the party of which that candidate is a representative must for the purpose of factor A be counted as two votes." — [LawLibrary PDF as at 5 Aug 2002](https://media.lawlibrary.org.za/media/legislation/298866/source_file/57c2031caec55db5/1998-117.pdf)
- Item 13(1)(b), verbatim: "If a ward candidate representing a party is elected unopposed, a vote cast by a voter registered in that ward for the party of which that candidate is a representative must for the purpose of paragraph (a) be counted as two votes." — same PDF
- Related surplus rule (for the remainder tie question): Item 13(2)(a) "that surplus must compete with similar surpluses accruing to any other party or parties, and any undistributed seat or seats must be awarded to the party or parties concerned in sequence of the highest surplus." and 13(2)(b) "If the surplus for two or more parties is equal the seat must be awarded to the party that obtained the highest number of valid votes." — same PDF

### Inferences
- The doubling applies only to votes cast by voters registered in the unopposed ward for the winning candidate's party; it does not apply to other parties' votes in that ward (the text speaks of the party the unopposed candidate represents).

### Gaps
- None for the text. I did not check whether any IEC report shows an unopposed-ward adjustment in practice.
