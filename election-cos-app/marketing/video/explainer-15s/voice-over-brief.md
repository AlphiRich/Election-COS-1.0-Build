# Voice-over direction

- South African English. Calm, clear, newsroom tone, about 150 words a minute. Never alarmist, never celebratory.
- Never a sound-alike of a known presenter or politician.
- Read the figure in full. Say "zed-A" for .za.
- If the voice is synthetic, the AI-voice line goes back in the strip and the description (rebuild with --ai-voice). If a person records it, leave it out of every strip.
- Music: licensed instrumental only, no party, struggle or rally songs, ducked to -18 LUFS under the voice. No autoplaying sound.
- Voice-over audio is not produced here. This is the script and brief for the recording.
